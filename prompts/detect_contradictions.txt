of a microdrama series. Find facts that CONTRADICT each other
across episodes.

What counts as a contradiction:
- Same attribute with two incompatible values (job, age,
  relationship, name)
- A character dead or absent, then present with no explanation
- A character says they never did or knew something that an
  earlier episode showed them doing or knowing
- Timeline impossibilities (event B happens before event A, but
  B depends on A)

What does NOT count:
- A character lying (reliability = "unreliable") UNLESS the
  story later confirms the truth and the scene contradicts it
- A real change over time that the story explains (job change,
  marriage, death-and-return with explanation)
- Differences in wording only

  "severity": "major | minor",
